What the model enforces

Seven rules, and every one of them can be made to bite

Interception, not input

Sunlight is not the scarce thing. gross = base × light × water × aim. Leave the light at full and take the base down to 20 units: the photons keep arriving in exactly the same quantity, and the stack still dies at grade 2.

Try: base breadth → 20, sunlight → 1.00

The base is not free

Leaves shade each other, so capture saturates while upkeep keeps climbing in a straight line. Past roughly 260 units a wider base makes less surplus, not more — and every extra unit past that pushes the harvest later into the season.

Try: base breadth 260, then 520 — watch surplus

Freedom at the bottom destroys aim at the top

Discipline sets both the share of the base pointed at the job and the variance of what arrives. Stability multiplies every conversion above grade 1: a jittery supply cannot be refined, only burned.

Try: free-form base

Fuel sets the ceiling

The converters never change. On thin input the same stack runs in the same order and halts partway up. Sourness here is not a defect — it is an unfinished ripening cascade, and it looks identical to a broken machine.

Try: thin season, or a drought at day 90

The bribe is unpayable early

Ripening is gated on a finished seed, and the dispersal signal goes as ripeness to the fourth power. Half-ripe is not half an offer — it is six per cent of one, so foragers pass the fruit by until the payload is done.

Watch the signal readout as the seed crosses 100%

Abandonment gets expensive

Drop the fruit on day 30 and you lose almost nothing. Drop it on day 150 and the sunk figure is a whole season of refinement. The tree runs this same calculation, and abscises under reserve stress.

Try: all-in on flesh, and watch it drop the fruit itself

One output does not decay

The ledger settles every season: leaves fall, sugar is spent, flesh rots. One line survives, and it starts an identical engine on ground the parent could never occupy.

Watch the ground map after a successful dispersal
